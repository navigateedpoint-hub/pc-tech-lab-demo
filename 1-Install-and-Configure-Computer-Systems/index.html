PC Tech Lab · Guided demo

Install and Configure Computer Systems

Build a PC from its parts, then install and check its operating system, one step at a time.

The demo always points to the next thing to do, so nobody gets lost and no detail is skipped.

Hands-on

The learner clicks the highlighted spot to do each step. Nothing else on the screen reacts, so the order is always right.

Slides

For teaching from the front. Press →, Space or a presentation clicker: the first press does the step, the next press moves on. ← goes back.

Jump anywhere

Pick a chapter from the menu at the top of a station. Each chapter starts exactly where the previous one ended. F gives full screen.
